## 1. What is the Customer Portal?

The Apex Customer Portal is a self-service web application for businesses that generate vegetable-origin waste oils — including restaurants, food manufacturers, fast food outlets, and refineries. Through the portal you can:

- Request waste oil pickups
- Track collection history and volumes
- Download environmental compliance certificates
- Submit SARS and ISCC compliance declarations

The portal is a **Progressive Web App (PWA)** — it can be installed directly to your phone or desktop without an app store.

### Supported Oil Types
- Used Cooking Oil (UCO)
- Gum Oil
- Winterized Oil
- Acid Oil

## 2. Getting Started

### Installing the App (Optional)

The portal works in any browser. To install it on your device:

1. Open [apex-customer-app.vercel.app](https://apex-customer-app.vercel.app) in your browser
2. On **Android**: tap the three-dot menu → *Add to Home screen*
3. On **iPhone**: tap the Share icon → *Add to Home Screen*
4. On **Desktop (Chrome)**: click the install icon in the address bar

### Registering an Account

New customers must register and await admin approval before accessing the portal.

1. Go to the app and click **Register your business**
2. Fill in **Step 1 — Business Details**:
   - Full name
   - Email address (used to log in)
   - Phone number
   - Business name
   - Business type (Restaurant, Food Manufacturer, Refinery, etc.)
   - Business registration number (optional)
   - Physical address
3. Fill in **Step 2 — Account Security**:
   - Choose a password (minimum 8 characters)
4. Click **Register**

> **Important:** After registering, your account will be **pending approval**. Apex Chem admin staff will review and activate your account. You will be able to log in once approved.

### Logging In

1. Enter your **email address** and **password**
2. Click **Sign In**

If you have forgotten your password, use the **Send Magic Link** button — a login link will be sent to your email.

| Card | What it shows |
|------|--------------|
| **Total Collected** | Cumulative litres/kg of waste oil collected to date |
| **Recent Collections** | Your last few pickups with dates and volumes |
| **Upcoming Pickup** | Next scheduled collection (if booked) |
| **Quick Actions** | Shortcut buttons to Request Pickup and view Collections |

## 4. Requesting a Pickup

To request a collection from Apex Chem:

1. From the Dashboard or navigation, tap **Request Pickup**
2. Select your registered bin from the dropdown, or choose **Manual Entry** to enter a bin serial number manually
3. If using manual entry, fill in:
   - Bin serial number
   - Bin type (Standard 120L, Large 240L, IBC 1000L, Drum 210L, etc.)
   - Urgency level: **Normal**, **Urgent**, or **Scheduled**
4. Add any notes for the driver (e.g. gate code, access instructions)
5. Click **Submit Request**

Your request will appear in **My Collections** with status *Pending*.

## 5. My Collections

This section shows your complete collection history.

### Status Meanings

| Status | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Pending** | Request submitted, waiting for driver assignment |
| **In Progress** | Driver is en route or currently collecting |
| **Completed** | Collection done, volumes recorded |
| **Verified** | Admin has verified and signed off the collection |

### What You Can See Per Collection

- Date and time
- Volume collected (litres or kg)
- Oil type
- Driver name
- Collection notes and any photos

## 6. Certificates

Apex Chem issues environmental compliance certificates for each verified collection. To access them:

1. Go to **Certificates** in the navigation
2. Search by certificate number or filter by date range
3. Click **Download PDF** next to the certificate you need

Certificates confirm the volume, type, and responsible disposal of your waste oil. They are useful for ESG reporting and regulatory submissions.

## 7. Compliance Forms

The portal allows you to submit two official compliance forms digitally.

### SARS VAT 264 Declaration

The VAT 264 is a South African Revenue Service declaration for the supply of second-hand goods (which includes waste oil).

**Where to find it:** Navigate to **Certificates → SARS VAT 264** tab

**Fields required:**
- Company name, registration number, VAT number, tax reference
- Seller/buyer details
- Description of goods (e.g. Used Cooking Oil)
- Volume and value of goods
- Reporting period

After completing the form, click **Submit Declaration**. A PDF copy will be generated for your records.

### ISCC Sustainability Declaration

The ISCC (International Sustainability & Carbon Certification) declaration is required for ESG compliance and biodiesel feedstock certification.

**Where to find it:** Navigate to **Certificates → ISCC** tab

**Fields required:**
- Certificate number and validity period
- Site information and GPS coordinates
- Feedstock type and volume (UCO, gum oil, winterized oil, acid oil)
- GHG (greenhouse gas) emission values
- Sustainability compliance checkboxes
- Audit information

## 9. Troubleshooting

| Problem | Solution |
|---------|----------|
| Cannot log in | Check your email and password. Use *Send Magic Link* to receive a login link by email |
| Account says "pending" | Your registration is awaiting admin approval — contact info@apexchemicals.co.za |
| Certificate not showing | Certificates are generated after admin verifies the collection. Allow 24–48 hours |
| App not loading | Check your internet connection and try refreshing. Clear browser cache if needed |
| Form submission fails | Ensure all required fields (marked with *) are filled in |
