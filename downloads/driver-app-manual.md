## 1. What is the Driver App?

The Apex Driver App is the field tool for Apex Chem collection drivers. It is designed to run on a phone or tablet and allows you to:

- See all jobs assigned to you
- Navigate to client collection sites
- Record oil collection volumes and types
- Upload photos of collections, payment slips, and supporting documents
- Submit completed collections for admin verification

The app is a **Progressive Web App (PWA)** and works offline. Jobs load when you have internet and key data is cached for use in the field.

---

## 2. Getting Started

### Installing the App

1. Open [apex-driver-app.vercel.app](https://apex-driver-app.vercel.app) in your phone browser
2. **Android:** tap the three-dot menu → *Add to Home screen*
3. **iPhone:** tap the Share icon → *Add to Home Screen*

The app will appear as an icon on your home screen and open full-screen.

### Registering an Account

Only Apex Chem drivers can register. Your account requires admin approval before you can log in.

1. Open the app and tap **Register**
2. Fill in your details:
   - Full name
   - Email address
   - Phone number
   - Vehicle type (Bakkie, Van, Truck, etc.)
   - Vehicle registration number
   - ID number
3. Upload your **ID document** (front and back photos)
4. Create a password
5. Tap **Register**

> **Your account will be pending approval.** Admin staff will review your documents and activate your account. You will be notified once approved.

### Logging In

1. Enter your email and password
2. Tap **Sign In**

## 3. Dashboard

The Dashboard is the first screen after login. It displays:

- **Jobs assigned today** — a count of today's pending jobs
- **Completed today** — how many jobs you have finished today
- **Recent activity** — a quick look at your last few jobs
- **Quick links** — tap to go directly to Jobs or Map

---

## 4. Jobs

### Viewing Your Jobs

Tap **Jobs** in the bottom navigation bar to see all jobs assigned to you. Each job card shows:

- Client name and address
- Bin serial number
- Job status (Pending, In Progress, Completed, Verified)
- Scheduled date/time (if applicable)

Tap any job card to open the **Job Detail View**.

### Job Status Filters

Use the filter buttons at the top of the Jobs screen to sort your list:

| Filter | Shows |
|--------|-------|
| **All** | Every job assigned to you |
| **Pending** | Jobs not yet started |
| **Completed** | Jobs you have submitted for verification |
| **Pending Verification** | Collections awaiting admin sign-off |
| **Verified** | Collections confirmed by admin |

## 5. Completing a Collection

### Job Detail View

When you open a job you will see:

- **Client name** and full address
- **Bin details** — serial number, bin type, bin size
- **Map** — showing the location of the client site
- **Navigate** button — opens Google Maps / Apple Maps for directions
- **Collection form** — to record the oil collected

### Oil Collection Form

Once you arrive at the site and collect the oil, fill in the form:

1. **Select the bin** — the bin is usually pre-populated from the job
2. **Volume collected** — enter the amount in litres
3. **Oil type** — select from:
   - Used Cooking Oil (UCO)
   - Gum Oil
   - Winterized Oil
   - Acid Oil
4. **Collection notes** — any observations about the collection (damaged bin, low volume, etc.)
5. **Photos** — take or upload at least one photo of the collection (see below)
6. Tap **Submit Collection**

The job status will change to *Completed* and admin will be notified for verification.

### Uploading Photos and Documents

The app supports uploading:

- **Collection photos** — camera capture or gallery upload
- **Supporting documents** — PDFs, Word files, Excel files, text/CSV
- Non-image files display with a document icon and filename

**To upload:**
1. Tap **Add Photo** or **Upload Document** in the collection form
2. Choose from camera or file picker
3. Multiple files can be uploaded per collection
4. Files can be dragged and dropped on tablet/desktop

### Payment Slips

If you paid the client for their oil:

1. In the collection form, scroll to **Payment Slip**
2. Tap **Upload Payment Slip**
3. Take a photo of the receipt or select from your gallery
4. The payment slip is attached to the collection record for admin review

## 6. Map & Navigation

Tap **Map** in the bottom navigation to see all your assigned jobs on a map.

- Each job appears as a pin on the map
- Tap a pin to see the job summary
- Tap **Navigate** to open directions in Google Maps or Apple Maps

> **Tip:** If the address does not load correctly, check the job detail for the full address and type it into Google Maps manually.

---

## 7. My Jobs (Collection History)

Tap **My Jobs** to see your complete collection history, not just today's jobs.

This view shows:
- All past collections with dates and volumes
- The status of each job
- Used the same status filters as the Jobs tab

Use this to track your overall collection record and check the outcome of any job submitted for verification.

## 8. Profile

## 9. Troubleshooting

| Problem | Solution |
|---------|----------|
| No jobs showing | Pull down to refresh. Ensure you are logged in with the correct account |
| Cannot submit collection | Ensure a volume is entered and at least one photo is attached |
| App not working offline | Open the app once with internet to cache your jobs, then it will work offline |
| Map not loading | Check your browser's location permissions — the app needs location access for the map |
| Photos not uploading | Check your internet connection. Try a smaller file or compress the image |
| Account says "pending" | Contact admin at info@apexchemicals.co.za to activate your account |
| Forgot password | Contact admin to reset — email info@apexchemicals.co.za |
