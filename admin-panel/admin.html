Bins Management

How bin management works

  1. Allocate a bin to a customer by clicking Add Bin. The bin is created in the bins table with the customer's customer_id.
  2. Click the QR button on a row to generate and download a printable QR code for that bin.
  3. Print the QR, stick it on the physical bin. The customer can scan it in the Customer App to see their bin, and the depot can scan it in the Aggregator App when receiving the bin.
  4. The Driver App sees the bin serial in the pickup job's notes and uses it for confirmation.

Depot QR Code Management

Two sets of QR codes: Oil Type QR codes (scan to auto-fill the oil type in the Depot app) and IBC Bin QR codes (scan to identify the physical bin).

How to create & use QR codes

  1. Find the QR code you need below (oil type or IBC bin number).
  2. Click the Download PNG button under it.
  3. Print the downloaded image and stick it on the physical drum / storage area / bin.
  4. In the Depot App → Inbound Receipt, tap Open Scanner and scan the printed QR.
  5. The oil type or bin fills in automatically — no typing needed.
